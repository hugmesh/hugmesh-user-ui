Getting started

Here's how to make the most of your account:

  • Set up your profile — choose a display name, a screen-reader name, pronouns, timezone and visibility.
  • Publish federation cards — share your own federation bundles with the network.
  • Manage self-blocks — mute or block accounts across Matrix, Mastodon, or internally.
  • Recommend an instance block — flag a problem instance for moderator review.
  • Hide yourself temporarily — self-silence to become invisible to others until a time you choose (you auto-reactivate, and it is not a ban).
  • Manage connected apps — review and revoke external apps and device sessions (needs an external-client role).
